Questions

Do I need to sign up?

No. Open the site and start your first lesson.

Where is my progress saved?

In the browser you study on. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, your progress won't come with you.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works in any modern phone or computer browser.

What level is it for?

Beginners, starting from kana. JLPT N5–N4.

How much time does it take each day?

A few minutes. Do your due reviews, and add a lesson when you have the energy.
